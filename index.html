WHY DIVE LOCAL

Scuba is more than
getting in the water.

Local dive shops create the infrastructure behind the sport: training, equipment, repairs, rentals, trips, mentorship, and community.

Fins on Feet is a website concept built around one simple idea: make it easier for divers to support the people who make diving possible.
